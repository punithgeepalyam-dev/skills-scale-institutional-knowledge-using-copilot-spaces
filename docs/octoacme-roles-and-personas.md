# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with QA/Quality Assurance Lead to define acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Collaborate with Tech Lead/Architecture Owner on technical design and code reviews
- Partner with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer on deployment and infrastructure requirements
- Receive requirements from Product Manager and Scrum Master support

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Stakeholder/Client Representative to understand business requirements and user needs
- Align with Project Manager on timeline and resource constraints
- Collaborate with Tech Lead/Architecture Owner on technical feasibility and roadmap sequencing
- Work with QA/Quality Assurance Lead to define quality standards and release criteria

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work closely with Scrum Master/Agile Coach on ceremony facilitation and process improvement
- Coordinate with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer on deployment schedules and infrastructure readiness
- Align with Product Manager on prioritization and roadmap changes
- Report status to Stakeholder/Client Representative and escalate risks/blockers

---

## QA/Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Quality Assurance Leads ensure product quality and reliability through systematic testing, quality standards, and defect management. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define acceptance criteria and quality gates.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategies and quality standards for the project
- Execute and coordinate testing activities across team
- Manage the defect lifecycle from identification to resolution
- Ensure compliance with quality requirements and acceptance criteria
- Establish quality metrics and reporting mechanisms
- Participate in release readiness assessments

### Goals
- Deliver zero-critical-defect releases
- Maintain high customer satisfaction and product reliability
- Reduce production incidents and rework
- Enable fast, confident deployments through quality assurance

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect tracking and status reports
- Quality metrics dashboards and trends
- Release readiness assessments
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Developers on acceptance criteria definition and test coverage
- Collaborate with Product Manager on quality standards and release criteria
- Work with Tech Lead/Architecture Owner on test strategy for complex components
- Coordinate with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer on test environment setup and deployment validation
- Support Project Manager with quality status reporting

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers own infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and operational reliability. They enable teams to deploy quickly and safely while maintaining system availability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines for automated testing and deployment
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and cloud resources
- Enable monitoring, observability, and alerting capabilities
- Support incident response and troubleshooting
- Ensure scalability, security, and disaster recovery planning
- Document infrastructure and deployment procedures

### Goals
- Enable rapid, safe deployments with minimal manual effort
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce mean time to recovery (MTTR) from incidents
- Support team productivity through infrastructure automation

### Typical Communication
- Deployment runbooks and release procedures
- Infrastructure documentation and architecture diagrams
- Operational dashboards and performance metrics
- Incident post-mortems and lessons learned
- Collaboration on infrastructure capacity and scaling needs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Developers on application deployment requirements and CI/CD integration
- Partner with Tech Lead/Architecture Owner on infrastructure design and scalability
- Support QA/Quality Assurance Lead with test environment provisioning and deployment validation
- Coordinate with Project Manager on deployment schedules and release windows
- Provide operational metrics to stakeholders

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on agile practices. They act as servant leaders to enable team productivity and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, daily standup, review, retrospective)
- Track and remove blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Foster continuous improvement and team collaboration
- Maintain sprint board and ensure transparency
- Support retrospective outcomes and action items

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Improve team collaboration and psychological safety
- Enable sustainable pace and reduce burnout
- Continuously improve processes and team performance

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremony facilitation and notes
- Impediment tracking and resolution status
- Retrospective outcomes and process improvements
- Team coaching and mentoring
- Visibility into team health and morale

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work closely with Project Manager on planning and risk management
- Support all team members in removing blockers and improving collaboration
- Facilitate communication between Product Manager and development team
- Ensure alignment with organizational and project goals
- Coach team on working effectively with Tech Lead and QA roles

---

## Tech Lead/Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Tech Leads/Architecture Owners provide technical direction, own architecture decisions, and ensure technical excellence. They balance long-term design with delivery speed and support team members in solving complex problems.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and system architecture
- Conduct design reviews and provide technical guidance
- Mentor engineers on best practices and technical topics
- Identify technical debt and propose solutions
- Make or facilitate architecture decisions with stakeholder input
- Ensure code quality and maintainability standards

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality and system scalability
- Reduce technical debt and improve system resilience
- Enable sustainable growth and team capability
- Support rapid, safe feature delivery

### Typical Communication
- Design documents and architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code review feedback and technical mentoring
- Architecture design reviews and trade-off analyses
- Technical debt prioritization and roadmap
- Design and capability discussions with team

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design reviews and technical mentoring
- Partner with Product Manager on feasibility and roadmap sequencing
- Work with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer on scalability and deployment architecture
- Support QA/Quality Assurance Lead with test strategy for complex components
- Advise Project Manager on technical risks and mitigation strategies

---

## Stakeholder/Client Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder/Client Representatives represent customer and business interests. They provide context on business requirements, success metrics, and user needs to guide product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Provide user context, feedback, and validation of solutions
- Communicate business impact and ROI of initiatives
- Escalate business-critical issues and priorities
- Represent customer perspective in decision-making
- Validate solutions meet user needs and business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure business value delivery and ROI
- Validate solutions meet user needs and expectations
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Facilitate alignment between technical delivery and business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user stories
- Feedback and solution validation
- Business metrics and success criteria
- Stakeholder updates and executive briefings
- User research and customer feedback insights

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Product Manager to translate business needs into product vision and priorities
- Provide feedback and validation to Developers and QA teams
- Work with Project Manager on timeline feasibility from business perspective
- Collaborate with Tech Lead/Architecture Owner on technical trade-offs impacting business
- Support Scrum Master in communicating business context to the team
